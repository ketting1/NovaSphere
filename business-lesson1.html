Lesson 1

Affiliate Marketing Fundamentals

What is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is promoting products or services and earning a commission when someone buys through your special link.

How It Works

1. Join an affiliate program. 2. Get your affiliate link. 3. Share content. 4. Earn commissions from sales.

Popular Platforms

ClickBank, Amazon Associates, Digistore24 and many other affiliate networks.
